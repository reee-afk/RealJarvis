# Haven Solutions — Service Catalog & Pricing

**Positioning:** Pain-led, not category-led. This internal doc keeps the vertical labels (Tech/Design/Combined) for your own planning — customer-facing pages should never show these labels.

**Pricing logic:**
- **Local (India) pricing** — competitive, cash-flow-friendly, positions you to close fast for portfolio/case studies.
- **UK pricing** — reflects UK market rates (GBP), used for cold outreach. Roughly 3–4x local INR-equivalent, standard for agencies selling into UK from lower-cost markets.
- Every price is a *starting* price — scope creep (extra pages, custom integrations) should trigger a change order, not silent absorption.

## Pricing Strategy Notes

1. **Anchor with the bundle.** Lead cold outreach with the Growth Starter Bundle or Full Launch Package, not individual line items — bundles feel like more value per pitch and raise average deal size.
2. **Foot-in-door, not race-to-bottom.** Landing Page (#1) and Print Collateral (#10) are cheap enough to get a "yes" from a skeptical local business owner, then upsell into the retainer once trust is built.
3. **Retainers are the real business.** One-time projects fund the runway; #7, #16, #17 are where recurring revenue and founder-market fit actually show up. Prioritize converting every project client into at least one retainer line.
4. **UK pricing isn't just "convert and multiply."** UK SMBs expect higher baseline quality signals (case studies, testimonials, a polished pitch deck) to justify GBP pricing — this is where your spec samples/live demos (already flagged as a gate item) matter most.
5. **Don't itemize on the client-facing site.** Keep this granularity internal/in proposals. The website should show pain-led entry points; pricing conversations happen after a discovery call, not on a public price list.
